What Is It

Schengen & European Visa Refusal — What Went Wrong?

European visa refusals typically stem from specific, fixable issues — insufficient financial proof, weak cover letters, missing documents, unclear travel purpose, or doubts about your intent to return to India. A refusal is not a permanent ban.

GM Overseas offers a detailed Visa Refusal Audit. We read your refusal letter closely, map each ground of refusal to specific documentation gaps, and build a targeted reapplication or formal appeal with evidence that directly addresses the embassy's concerns.

  • Line-by-line refusal letter analysis — we explain exactly why you were refused
  • Identification of missing or weak documents in your original application
  • Strengthened cover letter addressing each ground of refusal
  • Rebuilding financial and ties-to-home-country evidence
  • Formal appeal filing where permitted (e.g., Germany, Netherlands)
  • Advice on optimal waiting period before reapplication
  • Covers Schengen, UK, and all national European visa refusals
