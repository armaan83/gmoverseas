What Is It

Long Stay National Visa & Permanent Residency in Europe

A long-stay national visa (Type D) is issued for stays exceeding 90 days and forms the foundation of your European settlement journey. It is tied to a purpose — work, study, family reunification, or retirement — and converts into a residence permit upon arrival.

GM Overseas advises Indian nationals on the full settlement pathway: from choosing the right country and visa category, to obtaining a residence permit and eventually permanent residency (PR). We serve professionals, retirees, investors, and families looking to make Europe their long-term home.

  • Long-stay national D-Visa application support for all purposes
  • Residence permit registration guidance on arrival in Europe
  • Permanent residency (PR) pathway planning and eligibility assessment
  • Retirement and passive income visa guidance (Portugal, Spain, Italy)
  • Investor visa and Golden Visa advisory (Portugal, Greece)
  • Family settlement — spouse, children, and parent inclusion
  • Pan-India service for all Indians planning long-term settlement

📋 Documents Required

01
Valid passport with minimum 2 years validity for long-stay applications
02

03

05

06
Police clearance certificate (PCC) from India
07
Medical fitness certificate (for certain countries)
08
Proof of no criminal record and background verification
