What Is It

India to Europe Talent Bridge — Verified & Work-Ready

European employers across IT, healthcare, hospitality, engineering, and skilled trades face a growing talent gap. India offers a vast, English-proficient, technically skilled workforce ready to fill these roles. GM Overseas is the bridge.

We handle everything from requirement analysis and talent sourcing to interview coordination, employment contract review, and full work visa processing for placed candidates. European employers get verified talent; Indian candidates get a trusted pathway to work abroad legally.

  • Skilled, semi-skilled, and professional talent sourcing from across India
  • Pre-screening, shortlisting, and reference verification
  • Interview coordination between employer and candidates
  • Employment contract review and guidance
  • Full work permit and D-Visa processing for placed candidates
  • Pre-departure briefing and cultural onboarding for candidates
  • Sectors: IT, healthcare, engineering, hospitality, manufacturing, finance
