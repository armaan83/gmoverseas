What Is It

Family Reunification & Dependent Visa for Europe

If your spouse, parent, or children are residing in Europe (on a work permit, student visa, or as citizens/residents), you may be eligible to join them through a Family Reunification Visa or Dependent Visa. The process and eligibility depend on the sponsor's status in Europe.

GM Overseas handles both short-term family visit visas (Type C Schengen) and long-term family reunification visas (Type D national visa) for all major European countries. We ensure the sponsor and applicant documentation is complete and strong, giving your application the best chance of approval.

  • Family visit visa (Schengen Type C) for short-term visits
  • Family reunification (Type D) for long-term residency in Europe
  • Spouse and dependent visa for Indian partners of EU residents
  • Parent visit visa to visit children settled in Europe
  • Joint application support for the whole family
  • Sponsor documentation guidance for the European resident
  • Pan-India service — we serve applicants from across India

📋 Documents Required

01
Valid passport for all applicants (min 6–12 months validity)
02
Proof of relationship — marriage certificate, birth certificates
03
Sponsor's residence permit, work permit or citizenship proof (from Europe)
04
Sponsor's financial proof — payslips, bank statements, employment contract
05
Sponsor's accommodation proof — rental agreement or property ownership
06
Travel insurance for the applicant (min €30,000 for Schengen)
07
Applicant's bank statements showing financial means
08
Cover letter from sponsor explaining intention and accommodation
09
Applicant's employment/leave NOC (if applicable)
