FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

+ How long does a Schengen visa take to process?

Schengen visa processing typically takes 15 calendar days, but during peak season (May-September), it can take up to 30 days. At GM Overseas, we help prepare your application well in advance to ensure timely processing.

+ Do you offer visa guarantee?

No legitimate consultancy can guarantee visa approval as the final decision rests with the embassy. However, with our 98% success rate and expert document preparation, we significantly improve your chances of approval.

+ What documents do I need for a German student visa?

Key documents include: university admission letter, proof of financial means (blocked account ~€11,208), health insurance, academic transcripts, language proficiency certificates, passport copies, and motivation letter. We provide a complete checklist in our resources.

+ Can you help with visa refusal appeals?

Absolutely. We specialize in reviewing refused applications, identifying weak points, preparing stronger documentation, and filing effective appeals. Many of our clients came to us after their first rejection.

+ What is your fee structure?

Fees vary depending on the service type and country. Student admissions start from ₹45,000, work permits from ₹75,000, and Schengen visas from ₹15,000. Book a free consultation for a detailed quote tailored to your case.

+ Do you help with accommodation in Europe?

Yes, we provide pre-departure guidance including accommodation options, banking setup, local SIM cards, and essential relocation tips. For students, we partner with housing providers in several countries.
