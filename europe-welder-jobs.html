Europe Work Visa for Indian Welders:
Job Vacancies & Visa Process

📅 Published: July 16, 2026 ⏱ Read Time: 8 mins

European countries are undergoing a massive industrial boom, particularly in heavy metal fabrication, shipyards, pipeline construction, and infrastructure projects. Due to a shrinking local working population, countries in the **Schengen Zone and Central Europe** are experiencing a severe shortage of skilled industrial professionals.

Among all technical trades, **welders are in the highest demand**. Countries like Croatia, Poland, Romania, Lithuania, and Germany have added welding to their official "shortage occupations list," opening direct pathways for Indian welders to secure work permits, earn attractive salaries, and relocate to Europe.

In this guide, we detail the salary expectations, required certifications, and step-by-step visa application process for Indian welders looking to move to Europe.

Why Do European Countries Recruit Indian Welders?

European employers value Indian tradesmen because of their strong work ethic, high manual dexterity, and familiarity with international welding standards. By hiring from India, European firms fill crucial labor gaps, while Indian welders get a chance to build an international career with premium benefits.

Required Welding Certifications and Skills

To qualify for a European work permit, you must be a skilled welder with at least 2 to 5 years of verified experience. Employers look for proficiency in specific welding processes:

  • TIG Welding (process 141): High demand for stainless steel and pipe welding (argon welding). Highly paid.
  • MIG/MAG Welding (process 131/135): Used extensively in structural steel fabrication and factory lines.
  • SMAW / MMA / Stick Welding (process 111): Used for heavy construction and outdoor pipeline welding.

Crucial Advantage: Holding international certifications such as **ISO 9606-1** (European Standard) or **AWS D1.1** (American Welding Society) makes you an elite candidate, leading to faster job offers and higher starting salaries.

Step-by-Step Recruitment & Visa Process

Step 1: The Trade Test (Practical Video)

Since welding is a manual skill, European employers will not hire you based on paper alone. You must undergo a practical trade test. You will be asked to perform specific welding positions (e.g., 3G, 4G, or 6G pipes) on carbon steel or stainless steel, which is filmed as a continuous video and sent to the employer for approval. GM Overseas coordinates these trade tests in certified testing booths in India.

Step 2: Employment Offer & Work Permit Approval

Once selected, the employer issues an Employment Contract. They will apply for your official government Work Permit with the local Ministry of Interior (MUP) in Croatia or the Voivodeship office in Poland. This takes about 4 to 8 weeks.

Step 3: Document Apostille & VFS Visa Stamping

While the permit is being processed, you must secure a Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) from your local Passport Seva Kendra in India. This PCC must be **Apostilled by the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA)**.
Once the work permit approval letter arrives from Europe, you submit your passport, contract, work permit, and apostilled PCC to the VFS Global visa center to get your **Type D National Work Visa** stamped.
