Netherlands Highly Skilled Migrant Visa (Kennismigrant) 2025: Salary, Points & How Indians Can Move Without an EU Blue Card

📅 Published: July 18, 2026 ⏱ Read Time: 8 mins

What if the fastest way into Europe was not Germany's Opportunity Card or an EU Blue Card at all — but a route where a single job offer from a recognised sponsor unlocks a residence permit in weeks, not months? For Indian IT engineers, data scientists, and tech managers, that route is the Netherlands Highly Skilled Migrant Visa (Kennismigrant) — and in 2025 it remains one of the most employer-friendly work permits in the EU.

In this guide we explain the 2025 salary thresholds, what "recognised sponsor" really means, who qualifies, and the step-by-step path from an Indian job offer to a Dutch residence permit — in plain language, with figures you can act on.

What Is the Highly Skilled Migrant Visa (Kennismigrant)?

The Kennismigrant scheme is a fast-track work-residence permit for skilled professionals whom the Dutch government wants to attract. The defining feature: if your employer is a recognised sponsor (erkend referent) registered with the Immigration and Naturalisation Service (IND), your application is processed on a priority basis — often within 2 to 4 weeks, versus the standard multi-month timeline for ordinary work permits.

Unlike the EU Blue Card, the Kennismigrant has no points grid and no fixed salary-multiple rule. You qualify by holding a recognised job above the salary threshold with a recognised sponsor. That simplicity is why Indian tech talent increasingly chooses the Netherlands over slower routes.

Why Indians Choose the Netherlands

The Dutch tech sector is starving for engineers. Indian professionals fill thousands of roles at recognised sponsors like ASML, Booking.com, Philips, and hundreds of scale-ups. English is an official workplace language, and the 30% ruling makes take-home pay attractive. The Kennismigrant is built precisely for this inflow.

The 2025 Salary Threshold (Mandatory)

You must be paid at or above the statutory income floor. For 2025 the thresholds are:

  • Aged 30 or over: at least €5,331 gross per month
  • Under 30: at least €3,909 gross per month
  • After orientation year (zoekjaar) or graduates from a Dutch university: reduced threshold of €2,989 gross per month

The salary must be paid by the recognised sponsor and meet the threshold before taxes. These figures are revised annually, so always confirm the current year's number with the IND before you sign.

Kennismigrant vs EU Blue Card

Both let you work in the Netherlands. The EU Blue Card requires a degree + salary at ~1.5× the national average and is portable across the EU. The Kennismigrant requires a recognised sponsor but is faster and has no degree mandate for some roles. Many Indians take the Kennismigrant first, then switch to a Blue Card later for EU mobility.

Who Is Eligible? (At a Glance)

  • A valid job offer from a recognised sponsor listed on the IND public register
  • Salary at or above the 2025 threshold for your age group
  • A relevant degree or, for some specialist roles, proven specialist experience
  • A signed employment contract or assignment letter stating the salary
  • Health insurance arranged in the Netherlands from day one

Step-by-Step: How Indians Apply from India

  1. Confirm the sponsor: Check your employer on the IND "recognised sponsors" public list. This single check decides your processing speed.
  2. Verify the salary: Ensure your gross monthly pay meets the 2025 threshold for your age.
  3. Employer files the application: The recognised sponsor submits the residence permit request to the IND (often with an expedited decision).
  4. Get your approval: Priority processing typically returns a decision in 2–4 weeks.
  5. Collect your MVV / entry: If required, collect the provisional residence permit (MVV) from the Dutch embassy / VFS in India.
  6. Register in the Netherlands: On arrival, register with your municipality (BRP), open a bank account, and arrange health insurance.
  7. Receive your residence card: The IND issues your physical residence permit — you are now legally working.

Bonus: The 30% Ruling

Many Kennismigrant arrivals qualify for the 30% tax ruling — up to 30% of your salary can be paid tax-free if you meet the distance and salary criteria. For Indian engineers on the threshold salary, this is a meaningful boost to take-home pay and a major reason the Netherlands beats other EU destinations on net income.
