Schengen Tourist Visa Explained

A Schengen Tourist Visa (Type C) allows Indian citizens to travel freely across 27 European countries for up to 90 days within any 180-day period. It covers leisure travel, family visits, and short-term cultural experiences.

With GM Overseas, you get a dedicated consultant who understands exactly what each embassy requires — from Germany and France to Italy and Spain. We have processed hundreds of successful Schengen applications for Indian applicants across all income profiles and travel histories.

  • Valid for all 27 Schengen member countries with a single visa
  • Single, double, or multiple-entry options available
  • Expert cover letter and itinerary drafting included
  • Embassy appointment booking handled by us
  • Interview preparation and mock sessions available
  • Pan-India support — we serve clients from all Indian cities

📋 Documents Required

01
Valid passport with minimum 6 months validity and 2 blank pages
02
Recent passport-size photographs (white background, biometric)
03
Travel insurance with minimum €30,000 coverage for Schengen area
04
Bank statements showing sufficient funds (last 3–6 months)
05
Confirmed return flight tickets and hotel/accommodation bookings
06
Income proof: ITR, salary slips, or business registration documents
07
Leave letter / NOC from employer (for salaried applicants)
08
Cover letter explaining travel purpose and itinerary
09
Travel history (previous visa copies if applicable)

Common Questions

Schengen Visa FAQs for Indian Applicants

Key documents include a valid passport (6+ months validity), recent photographs, travel insurance (min €30,000 cover), bank statements (last 3–6 months), hotel bookings, flight itinerary, income tax returns, and a leave letter from your employer. GM Overseas provides a personalised checklist based on your specific profile.
Standard processing takes 15 working days from the date of biometric submission. With GM Overseas ensuring your file is complete and error-free before submission, delays are significantly reduced. We also advise on the earliest application window (up to 6 months before travel).
The official Schengen visa fee is €80 (approximately ₹7,200) for adults and €40 for children aged 6–12. Children under 6 are free. This fee is payable directly at VFS or the embassy and is non-refundable regardless of outcome. Our consultancy fee is separate and transparently disclosed.
Apply to the embassy of your primary destination (where you'll spend the most days). If spending equal time in multiple countries, apply to the country of first entry. GM Overseas helps you strategise embassy selection to maximise approval chances based on your profile and travel history.
Low balance is a common reason for refusal, but it doesn't automatically disqualify you. GM Overseas advises on how to structure your financial documents, sponsorship letters, and supporting evidence to build the strongest possible case even with moderate funds.
