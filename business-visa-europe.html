What Is It

Schengen Business Visa for Indian Professionals

A Schengen Business Visa (Type C) allows Indian professionals to travel across Europe for business activities — attending conferences, client meetings, trade exhibitions, training programs, and signing commercial agreements.

With GM Overseas, we handle the complete documentation package including a business-specific cover letter, invitation letter verification, and evidence of commercial ties — precisely what European embassies require for business applications.

  • Valid for attending conferences, trade fairs, meetings & training programs
  • Business invitation letter verification and guidance
  • Strong cover letter drafting for business purpose
  • Chamber of Commerce documents preparation
  • Multiple-entry visa strategy for frequent business travellers
  • Pan-India service — consult from anywhere in India

📋 Documents Required

01
Valid passport with minimum 6 months validity
02
Business invitation letter from European company/conference
03
Company registration documents (GST, incorporation cert)
04
Bank statements — personal and/or company (last 6 months)
05
ITR for last 2–3 years and business financials
06
Travel insurance with minimum €30,000 Schengen coverage
07
Hotel bookings and confirmed flight itinerary
08
Cover letter explaining business purpose and ties to India
09
Visiting card and company letterhead documents
