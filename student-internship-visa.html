European Internship Visa — What You Need to Know

A student internship in Europe requires the correct visa and work authorisation depending on the host country. This may be a Student Intern Visa (for enrolled students), a Trainee/Intern Work Permit (for graduates), or a Short-Stay Work Visa depending on duration and type.

GM Overseas assists with internship placement in Germany, France, Netherlands, Italy, and more — combined with the correct visa documentation to ensure you're legally authorised to work. We also assist with post-internship visa extensions and job-seeker visas.

  • Internship placement assistance in Germany, France, Netherlands, Italy and more
  • Internship visa and work permit documentation support
  • Cover letter, CV formatting and interview preparation
  • Host company verification and internship agreement review
  • Guidance on paid vs unpaid internship visa requirements
  • Support for post-internship visa extension or job-seeker visa
  • Pan-India service for students from all universities
